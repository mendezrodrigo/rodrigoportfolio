Method

Observe first. Build meaning. Make it legible. Respect culture.

Observe

Look at behavior, symbols, constraints, incentives, rituals, data, and silence before forcing a framework onto the room.

Build

Turn research into prototypes, narratives, timelines, strategy maps, podcasts, story worlds, or useful systems.

Translate

Make complex ideas legible across cultures, disciplines, and stakeholders without flattening their meaning.

Respect

Treat culture as infrastructure: invisible when ignored, decisive when understood, dangerous when simplified.
