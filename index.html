Working Philosophy

Observe first. Build meaning. Make it legible. Respect culture.

Strategy should feel like a meditation: silent, precise, and ready for something dangerous to become useful.

Intentional AttentionLook at behavior, symbols, constraints, incentives, rituals, data, and silence before forcing a framework onto the room.
The Why Is EverythingTurn research into prototypes, narratives, timelines, strategy maps, podcasts, story worlds, or useful systems.
Keep it AuthenticMake complex ideas understandable across cultures, disciplines, and stakeholders without flattening their meaning.
Lead With HumilityTreat culture as infrastructure: invisible when ignored, decisive when understood, dangerous when simplified.
